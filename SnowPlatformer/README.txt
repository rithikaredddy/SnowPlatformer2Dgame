SNOW PLATFORMER (Unity 6, 2D)
1. Copy this whole SnowPlatformer folder into your project's Assets folder.
2. Wait for Unity to finish compiling (spinner bottom-right).
3. Top menu: Snow Platformer > Build Level Scene
4. Press Play. Move: A/D or arrows. Jump: Space/W. Restart after win/lose: R.
All art is original and generated for this project. Scripts are prefixed "Snow" so they won't clash with your tutorial scripts.
